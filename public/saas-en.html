Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No. You get 14 days free with everything included, up to 8 barbers, without entering any card. If you don't subscribe when it ends, your page stops taking online bookings, but your data is kept.

How do I pay?

Online with Mercado Pago, from the Plan tab in your dashboard. It renews automatically every month, in Mexican pesos (MXN). If you need an invoice (CFDI), ask our support team.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from your dashboard and with no penalties. You won't be charged again and you keep the service until the end of the month you paid for.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. They book from your page on any phone. If they want, they can install it as an app on their home screen.

How do reminders reach my clients?

By WhatsApp. In your dashboard, each appointment's reminder is already written with the date, time and barber, and you send it with one tap. There's no per-message cost.

How many barbers can I have?

Up to 3 on the Basic plan and up to 8 on Pro. During the free trial, up to 8. Each barber gets their own account and only sees their own work.

What will my page's address be?

agendabarberias.xyz/your-barbershop, using the link you choose when you sign up. You can add it to your Instagram, to Google and to the QR poster.

Is the app available in English?

Not yet. The sign-up form (in Spanish), the owner dashboard and the booking page your clients see are currently in Spanish. This page is in English so you can see what it does before you start.
